24 Hours of Building

  • 08:00 AM – 09:00 AM

    Check-in & Breakfast Networking

    Badge pickup, kit collection, and team formation coffee corner.

  • 09:00 AM

    Opening Ceremony & Sprint Kickoff · Hacking Officially Begins

    Welcome address by GDG Leads; 24-hour hacking window officially starts!

  • 11:30 AM – 12:30 PM

    Mentor Clinic 1: Problem Validation & Architecture

    Technical architecture reviews with GDG mentor crew.

  • 01:30 PM – 02:30 PM

    Lunch & Light Lightning Talks

    Sample campus lunch served, 5-min community demos.

  • 05:00 PM – 06:30 PM

    Mentor Clinic 2: Mid-Sprint Checkpoint

    Testing API integrations and verifying core problem scope.

  • 08:30 PM – 09:30 PM

    Dinner & Fireside Chat

    Evening fuel and guest AMA with open-source contributors.

  • 07:30 AM – 08:30 AM

    Morning Chai & Final Stretch

    Light breakfast and code freeze countdown.

  • 09:00 AM

    Code Freeze & Project Submission

    All commit pushes and demo video submissions closed. Exactly 24 hours of hacking complete!

  • 09:30 AM – 12:30 PM

    Demo Fair & Jury Evaluation

    Teams present live prototypes at booth stations (3 min demo + 2 min Q&A).

  • 12:30 PM – 01:30 PM

    Lunch & Jury Deliberation

    Jury scoring collation and community voting.

  • 02:00 PM – 03:00 PM

    Awards Ceremony & Closing Note

    Track winners announcement, trophy distribution, and group photo.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before registering for BuildSprint 2026.

No prior experience is necessary! BuildSprint is beginner-friendly. We run introductory ideation workshops and provide 1-on-1 mentor desks throughout the sprint to help first-time participants design, code, and deploy functional prototypes.

No, you can register individually. Teams must have between 2 to 4 members. If you don't have a team yet, you can connect with other participants in our community channels or join our dedicated team-matching mixer on Saturday morning before hacking kicks off.

Yes, BuildSprint 2026 is 100% free of charge! Thanks to our sample community sponsors, meals, snacks, high-speed Wi-Fi, swag kits, and workshop access are all provided at zero cost for all selected participants.

Yes, BuildSprint is hybrid. Online participants can submit Git repositories, join live mentor breakout rooms on Discord, stream ceremonies on YouTube, and present during the demo evaluation remotely.

On-site participants should bring a laptop, charger, student or government-issued ID card, and any specialized hardware (e.g., IoT microcontrollers) you plan to use. Ahead of time, make sure your development environment and Git tools are set up.
